Thesaurus for Lego Categories: Description back to ToC

The thesaurus was created to provide a clear and hierarchical classification system for LEGO products and MOCs (My Own Creations), addressing the limitations of existing taxonomies on platforms like Rebrickable. Drawing inspiration from LEGO's "themes" and "interests" categories, the thesaurus refines these classifications to better suit user-generated MOCs, which often do not align with official LEGO sets. It introduces a more structured, organized approach to categorize LEGO-related content, expanding on existing categories and adding new ones.
